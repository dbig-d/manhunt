Manhunt: 3 style directions

The same two screens, the lobby and a seeker mid-hunt, drawn in three different styles. Pick one, or mix them (e.g. "Night Ops colors with Field Kit's big text"). The maps here are hand-drawn stand-ins; the real game uses a live street map styled to match.

Direction 1

Night Ops

A dark tactical HUD. Feels like a heist or a spy movie, with neon on near-black and a radar sweep on the map.

Feels like
Splinter Cell, a military radar, a hacker terminal
Best at
Atmosphere and tension, especially at dusk or night. Easy on battery with OLED screens.
Trade-off
Harder to read in bright midday sun. Can feel serious rather than fun.
Type
Chakra Petch (headings) + JetBrains Mono (numbers)
Base Seeker Hider Safe zone
Direction 2

Playground

Bright, chunky and cartoony. Thick outlines, hard drop shadows and bouncy markers. A party game you play outside.

Feels like
Among Us, Fall Guys, Brawl Stars menus
Best at
Fun and friendliness. Big rounded buttons are easy to hit while running.
Trade-off
Less tension and suspense. Busier screens.
Type
Fredoka (everything)
Base Seeker Hider Safe zone Accent
Direction 3

Field Kit

High-contrast and bold, like sports gear or a hi-vis jacket. Black on white, huge numbers, safety orange and blue.

Feels like
Nike Run Club, Strava, transit signage
Best at
Readability. Built for glancing at your phone mid-sprint in full sunlight.
Trade-off
The plainest of the three. Less personality unless we add it in sound and animation.
Type
Archivo (wide, heavy weights)
Base Seeker Hider Safe zone
